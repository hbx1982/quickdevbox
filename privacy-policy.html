Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

At QuickDevBox.com, accessible from https://quickdevbox.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by QuickDevBox and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Processing Guarantee (Zero Data Retention)

All developer and text utilities offered on QuickDevBox.com execute 100% within your local web browser engine using JavaScript. Your input data, tokens, secrets, or payloads are never transmitted to, stored on, or processed by remote servers.

2. Log Files

QuickDevBox follows a standard procedure of using log files provided by our CDN host (Cloudflare). The information collected by log files includes IP addresses, browser type, ISP, date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

QuickDevBox uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences and accessed pages to optimize the user experience.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site using DART cookies to serve ads based upon visits to www.com.com and other internet sites. Visitors may opt out via https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our Privacy Policy, contact us via email at support@quickdevbox.com.
